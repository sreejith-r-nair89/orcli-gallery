Face, hair and background contents are new; the reference photo itself is never given to the model. Krea2 turbo + OpenPose LoRA 0.85 + phonephoto / realistic-skin LoRAs, 10 steps, then a skin-texture refine pass (1.5× upscale, denoise 0.22); framing matched to the reference by person segmentation, whole head always kept. Head turn and tilt are measured on the reference (face landmarks) and written into the prompt; each result is measured the same way and re-rendered with a new seed when its head angle is clearly off. Skin texture is matched at the delivered size. 22 sets. All generated people are AI-generated.
