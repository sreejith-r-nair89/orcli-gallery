Mediums — Reference → Generated

Each set: reference photo → skeleton taken from it → a new synthetic person with the same pose, framing, clothing, lighting and type of background, finished to the reference's colour, sharpness and grain at the reference's own size → the same image at full size. Face, hair and background contents are new; the reference photo itself is never given to the model. Krea2 turbo + OpenPose LoRA 0.85 + phonephoto / realistic-skin LoRAs, 10 steps, then a skin-texture refine pass (1.5× upscale, denoise 0.22); framing matched to the reference by person segmentation, whole head always kept. Head turn and tilt are measured on the reference (face landmarks) and written into the prompt; each result is measured the same way and re-rendered with a new seed when its head angle is clearly off. Skin texture is matched at the delivered size. 10 sets. All generated people are AI-generated.
